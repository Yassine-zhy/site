PROCESSING
   Mono (channels averaged), resampled to 32000 Hz (soxr), leading silence
   removed (5 ms kept before the onset), trimmed (sustains 4.5 s / 0.35 s fade,
   short notes 2.5 s / 0.15 s fade, harp 3 s, tubular bells 5 s, percussion up
   to 5 s, timpani roll 4 s, gong 6 s), peak-normalised towards -1 dBFS with
   at most +12 dB of gain, encoded as OGG Vorbis (libvorbis, quality 3).
   MIDI numbers in manifest.json are the SOUNDING pitch of each recording
   (both libraries name most folders one octave below the sounding pitch).
